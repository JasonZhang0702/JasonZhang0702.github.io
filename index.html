About Me

I am a first-year Master's student majoring in Computer Science @ Tianjin University (TJU), advised by Prof. Bo Wang. Before that, I obtained my B.S. in Software Engineering @ Guangdong University of Foreign Studies (GDUFS), under the supervision of Prof. Xinying Qiu. Currently, I am visiting in iNLP Lab @ Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), supervised by Prof. Wenxuan Zhang. I also have the pleasure of working with Shaoyang Xu and Yanlin Fu.
My research focuses on natural language processing, large language models, and trustworthy AI, with the goal of making AI alignment with both human values and real-world environments measurable, interpretable, and externalizable.

  • Measurable — “What gets measured gets improved.” [Alignment but Flattening]
  • Interpretable — “Understanding is the first step toward improvement.” [Self-debiasing Neurons]
  • Externalizable — “Actions speak louder than words.” [Work in Progress]
I am actively seeking industry internship opportunities. If you think my background could be a good fit, feel free to reach out at audbut0702@163.com.
